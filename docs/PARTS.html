The Sabrent NT-P10G is a documented laptop adapter (manufacturer price was $129.99 before tax); this USB4 retailer example was listed at $99.95 before checkout. A used X540-T1 example was listed at $39.99 with free shipping before tax. A used HPE 560FLR-SFP+ example was near $20. A 75 ft pure-copper Cat6A example was $20.99 each before shipping and tax. These listings can change or sell out, and 75 ft works only if it actually reaches with slack.

Check before checkout: the HP's FlexibleLOM socket and current adapter, desktop full-height or low-profile bracket, Windows drivers, both actual cable routes, the switch's copper module compatibility, and the final tax/shipping total. Cisco's published SFP-10G-T-X 10G reach is 30 m over Cat6A/Cat7; do not assume a 100 ft cable meets a 30 m rating. The X540-T1 end supports up to 100 m Cat6A, so the switch module limits that example link.

Already owned and reused: Cisco 3850-24XS, matched optical modules and short rack fiber, two compatible 10G copper SFP+ switch modules, four compatible 1G copper SFP switch modules, four Cat5e Dell patch cables, six 300 GB 2.5-inch HDDs, one 512 GB SSD, 64 GB USB stick and drive trays/adapters (assumed owned). The photographed ImageStream PCIE-ETH-GIGX6 is set aside until its live PCI ID and link speed are confirmed. No Dell PCIe NIC, CPU upgrade, RAM upgrade, 40G module, or Raspberry Pi part is in this cart.
